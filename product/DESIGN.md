# Design System: ลูน่า เฮิร์บ

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

Persuade mode: a fictional single-product page arranged like a botanical product label. The product name, size, example price and LINE action appear before the photograph, followed by a compact formulation list and a short illustrated routine. The page labels the formula and photographs as examples and makes no customer-proof claims.

## Visual language

- Pale leaf `#e7eee0`, paper `#f7f8f1`, ink `#29392b`, and muted botanical rules `#82937c` give the page a light apothecary character. Vivid `#06c755` is reserved for LINE order actions.
- Self-hosted Anuphan Bold provides loopless display headings; Noto Sans Thai supplies readable text and controls. Body text is at least 16px with 1.7 line-height; headings use 1.35. Thai text is not tracked or transformed.
- A typographic offer sits above a wide photographic crop, followed by an unboxed ingredient list and a single routine image. The light footer carries the demo disclosure and photo credits.
- Banner and navigation share a sticky wrapper; anchor clearance and a skip link support keyboard navigation. Focus rings remain visible on light surfaces and LINE controls. Reduced motion is respected.
- On mobile, the product and offer precede a capped photo, and a compact bottom bar retains the LINE action without covering the footer.

## Assets and integrity

The photos are illustrative, licensed Pexels assets documented in CREDITS.md. Images below the fold load lazily; the hero image receives fetch priority. The Meta and TikTok pixel examples remain fully commented out.
