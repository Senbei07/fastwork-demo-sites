# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local neighborhood residents, weekend cyclists, remote workers, and visitors seeking a relaxed, authentic canal-side coffee and food experience in Khlong San, Bangkok. On mobile, visitors want quick access to opening hours, directions/map, LINE chat, and phone calls.

## Product Purpose

Serve as the digital storefront and welcoming face of "บ้านกาแฟริมคลอง" (Baan Kafe Rim Klong), conveying the warm, unhurried hospitality of a riverside wooden home turned cafe, presenting the complete menu with clear pricing, and making arrival effortless.

## Positioning

An authentic canal-side community cafe with slow-roasted Thai coffee beans and homemade Thai treats—not an industrial third-wave chain or an over-commercialized tourist trap.

## Operating Context

Visitors often discover the cafe on mobile while traveling or planning weekend outings, looking for opening status ("Is it open right now?"), the exact location along the canal, and how to reach the staff via LINE or phone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML on Cloudflare Pages, zero external CDNs, 100% self-hosted assets.
- Mobile PageSpeed 90+ in all categories.
- Must preserve: Hero, full menu with prices across all categories (Coffee, Non-coffee, Bakery), hours table, lazy Google Maps embed facade, LINE add-friend action (`https://line.me/R/ti/p/@demo-cafe`), phone call action (`tel:0812345678`), demo banner at top, footer demo disclaimer, and complete meta/OG tags.
- Non-negotiable demo constraints: clear "เว็บตัวอย่าง (Demo)" label, fictional branding only, real Unsplash/Pexels photos with photo credits in CREDITS.md and footer.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: บ้านกาแฟริมคลอง
- Tone: Warm, welcoming, grounded, gentle, unhurried (อบอุ่น ชุมชน เรียบง่าย ริมสายน้ำ).
- No generic AI marketing buzzwords ("ทำไมต้อง...", "ระดับพรีเมียม", "ที่สุดแห่ง...").
- Grounded Thai home-style hospitality.

## Evidence on Hand

- Complete beverage and bakery menu with realistic pricing in Baht (THB).
- Operating hours (Mon–Fri 07:00–20:00, Sat–Sun 07:00–21:00, Public holidays 08:00–20:00).
- Location: 123 ซอยริมคลอง 5 ถนนเจริญนคร คลองสาน กรุงเทพฯ 10600.
- LINE Official Account and phone contact info.

## Product Principles

1. **Feels like a real canal-side home, not a software template**: The digital presence mirrors the physical space—warm timber, natural canal light, peaceful rhythm.
2. **Menu as printed menu board/list**: Clear, scannable categories with descriptions and prices, not repetitive grid cards with icons.
3. **Respect user connection & device**: Zero remote third-party scripts, sub-500KB total transfer, self-hosted subset fonts, click-to-load map.
4. **Immediate mobile utility**: Hours, call, and LINE actions are always one tap away without blocking the reading experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast on all text and interactive elements.
- Semantic HTML with landmark roles, skip links, table captions, and accessible SVGs.
- Thai typography: 1.6–1.8 line height for body, 1.3–1.4 for headings, minimum 16px body, no clipping of tone marks or vowels.
