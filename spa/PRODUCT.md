# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Urban working professionals, wellness seekers, and couples in Bangkok seeking restorative bodywork, traditional Thai massage therapies, and natural facial skincare in a quiet, private sanctuary away from urban overstimulation.

## Product Purpose

Serve as the digital front door and primary booking conversion channel for ณิชา เวลเนส (Nicha Wellness), offering calm reassurance, transparent treatment pricing, and a simple online booking inquiry flow.

## Positioning

A peaceful, grounded day spa rooted in authentic Thai healing traditions and pure natural botanicals, distinguished by unhurried care, certified master practitioners, and private suites without pretentious luxury or clinical austerity.

## Operating Context

Visitors predominantly arrive via mobile browsers (via social media links, LINE recommendations, or local search), often during stressful workdays or evenings. The primary job is understanding services/prices quickly and requesting an appointment slot with minimal cognitive friction.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- High-performance static HTML/CSS/JS deployed on Cloudflare Pages.
- Booking inquiries submitted directly to Supabase REST endpoint (`/rest/v1/bookings`) with PDPA compliance consent and honeypot protection.
- Strict self-contained deployment: zero external CDN scripts/stylesheets/fonts, all assets self-hosted.
- Fictional business demo label "เว็บตัวอย่าง (Demo)" must remain prominent.
- Mobile Lighthouse score target 90+ (target 100).

## Brand Commitments

- Brand Name: ณิชา เวลเนส (Nicha Wellness).
- Tone of Voice: Serene, gracious, respectful, understated Thai hospitality.
- Avoid generic spa clichés (no gold gradients, no sage+cream formulas, no floating lotus clip-art).
- Transparent pricing in Thai Baht (฿) with clear session durations.

## Evidence on Hand

- Core service offerings: Thai Traditional Massage (500฿/hr), Aromatherapy (700฿/hr), Facial Treatment (800฿/session), Herbal Steam Room (400฿/45min), Reflexology Foot Massage (450฿/hr), Couple's Harmony Package (2,200฿/couple/2hr).
- Real client testimonials with authentic sentiment.
- Operating hours (Mon-Fri 10:00-20:00, Sat-Sun 09:00-21:00) and Sukhumvit 39 location.

## Product Principles

1. **Quiet Sanctuary**: The interface should lower blood pressure; whitespace and pacing embody the spa's calming ethos.
2. **Tactile Truth**: Use real, tactile photography (herbal compresses, natural linens, warm wood, skilled hands) instead of artificial graphics or staged models.
3. **Transparent Honesty**: Clean, legible pricing and clear booking expectations without hidden fees or bait-and-switch deals.
4. **Frictionless Action**: The booking form is the centerpiece; every path naturally leads to calm, confident reservation.
